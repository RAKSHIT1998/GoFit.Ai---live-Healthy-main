# Privacy Policy

**GoFit.Ai – Live Healthy**
*Last Updated: March 18, 2026*

## 1. Introduction

GoFit.Ai ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
- **Profile Data**: Age, gender, height, weight, fitness goals, and dietary preferences.
- **Health & Fitness Data**: Workout logs, meal scans, water intake, sleep data, and step counts.
- **Photos**: Food images you take for meal scanning (processed for nutritional analysis).

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, and app version.
- **Usage Data**: Features used, session duration, and interaction patterns (anonymized).
- **HealthKit Data**: With your explicit permission, we read health metrics from Apple HealthKit.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect location data.
- We do not collect contacts or call logs.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide personalized workout and meal recommendations.
- Analyze food photos to estimate nutritional content.
- Track your fitness progress and goals.
- Improve app performance and features.
- Send optional push notifications (with your consent).

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 AI-Powered Analysis
We use AI services (OpenAI GPT-4o) to analyze food photos and generate recommendations. When you scan a meal:
- Your food photo is sent securely over HTTPS.
- The AI processes the image to identify food items and estimate nutrition.
- Photos are not permanently stored by the AI provider.
- No personally identifiable information is sent with photos.

### 4.2 Backend Services
- **MongoDB Atlas**: Stores your account and fitness data securely.
- **Render.com**: Hosts our backend API servers.
- **Apple StoreKit**: Handles in-app purchases and subscriptions.

### 4.3 Analytics
We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
- Sensitive data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption.
- On-device data (meal history, training data) stays on your device unless you explicitly sync it.

## 6. Apple HealthKit

- HealthKit data is only accessed with your explicit authorization.
- We read: steps, active calories, workouts, heart rate, and sleep data.
- We write: workout sessions you log through the app.
- HealthKit data is **never** shared with third parties or used for advertising.
- HealthKit data is **never** sold to data brokers.

## 7. Your Rights & Controls

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data at any time through the app.
- **Export** your data (Profile → Export Data).
- **Delete** your account and all associated data (Profile → Delete Account).
- **Opt-out** of AI features and use manual entry instead.
- **Disable** push notifications at any time in Settings.
- **Revoke** HealthKit permissions through iOS Settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

GoFit.Ai is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

## 9. Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- When you delete your account, all data is permanently removed within 30 days.
- On-device cached data is removed immediately upon app deletion.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
