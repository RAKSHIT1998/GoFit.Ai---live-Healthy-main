## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I scan a meal?
Open the app → tap the Scan tab → take a photo of your food. The AI will identify items and estimate nutritional content.

### How accurate is the meal scanner?
The AI provides estimates based on visual analysis. For best results, take clear photos with good lighting. You can always edit the results before logging.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to **Settings** on your iPhone → tap your name → **Subscriptions** → select GoFit.Ai → **Cancel Subscription**.

### How do I delete my account?
Open GoFit.Ai → **Profile** → scroll to bottom → **Delete Account**. All your data will be permanently removed.

### Does the app work offline?
Yes! Workout tracking, manual meal logging, and viewing your history all work offline. Meal scanning requires an internet connection for AI analysis.

### How do I connect Apple Health?
Open GoFit.Ai → **Profile** → toggle **Health Sync** on. You'll be prompted to grant HealthKit permissions.

### Is my data safe?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit. We don't sell your data. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details.
